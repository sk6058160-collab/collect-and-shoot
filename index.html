How to Play

  • Hold your phone in landscape. Left stick moves your hero.
  • FIRE shoots your gun. Collect guns to make it stronger.
  • SLASH swings a sword up close. Collect swords to unlock and upgrade it.
  • BOW (teer kaman): hold to draw, release to fire. A longer draw deals more damage and flies farther. Arrows arc through the air and stick where they land.
  • Pick up powers for heal, shield, overdrive, or a nova blast — then tap POWER.
  • There are no coins or gems. Score only comes from defeating enemies and bosses.
  • Waves get harder. Every few waves, a giant boss enters the arena.
