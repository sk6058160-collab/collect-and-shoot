# Collect and Shoot v2.1

A landscape 3D open-world arena game for iPhone. Play in Safari, or add it to your Home Screen for full-screen, offline play.

**Play:** [Open the game](https://sk6058160-collab.github.io/collect-and-shoot/)

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari (if you already installed an older version, reopen the site so it can update).
2. Turn your phone sideways (landscape).
3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

## What's new in v2.1
- Bright daytime look: clear blue sky, soft clouds, and warm sunlight.
- Ruined Court is sunny sandstone. Ember Field keeps lava in daylight. Mist Pass is a green forest meadow with light haze.
- Enemies are detailed low-poly creatures (armored goblins, winged imps, stone golems, horned beasts) with walk, attack, hit, and death animations. Bosses have more detail and stay clearly visible.

## How to play
- Left stick moves your hero.
- **FIRE** shoots your gun. Collect guns to upgrade it.
- **SLASH** swings a sword (collect a sword first).
- **BOW** (teer kaman): hold to draw, release to fire. A longer draw does more damage and flies farther. Arrows arc and stick where they land.
- Pick up **powers** (heal, shield, overdrive, nova), then tap POWER.
- Score comes from defeating enemies and bosses — there are no coins or gems.
- Boss lairs are marked on the minimap. Walk in to start the fight.
